MarineSense Watch Log is installed.

START
  Start menu > MarineSense Watch Log
  A black window opens - keep it open while you use the log.
  Your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:5080/

PRACTISE FIRST
  Start menu > Watch Log - Training (sample data)
  Separate copy with invented readings. Nothing touches the real log.

GIVE FEEDBACK
  Press the yellow "Feedback" button on any screen and write your notes.
  When finished: Feedback > Export notes, and send the text file
  (it is saved in your Downloads folder).

Your data stays on this PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MarineSense\WatchLog.
Uninstalling the app keeps that data folder.

The full Reviewer guide is in the Start menu.
